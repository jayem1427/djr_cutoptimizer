Spindle speed, feed, removal rate, kc, power and torque follow Sandvik Coromant’s milling formulas. Average chip thickness uses the exact side-milling form, which matches Sandvik’s approximation at light stepovers. Deflection (cantilever with an equivalent diameter of 0.8 × D) and the force curve are estimates.

Wrought aluminium kc1 is calibrated to Sandvik CoroPlus ToolGuide (6061, 88 HB: 6 mm 4-flute, ae 1.67, ap 15, 24,000 rpm → 1.82 kW). Cast aluminium is scaled by the same factor. Other materials use reference values that have not been checked against Sandvik.

The force curve is the exact integral of the tangential chip load along a helical flute. Force slope is the steepest rise in force per degree of rotation; it doesn’t depend on rpm. Near a whole multiple of the flute pitch, slope is taken as the worst within ±0.05 mm of depth, because the cancellation there only works just below the pitch. The optimizer uses nominal engagement, and adaptive toolpaths go above it in corners, so leave margin on the force limit. Forces are tangential only; the resultant is typically 1.1–1.3× higher.

Planning values only. Verify against your tooling and setup.
